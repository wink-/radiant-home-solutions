Nail holes, dings, and popped trim are tiny jobs — but they're the ones that make a home feel worn. We patch, paint, and tighten everything back into shape.

## What We Handle

- Small hole patching (nail holes to fist-size)
- Touch-up painting
- Loose trim and molding
- Cabinet door adjustments
- Drawer slide repairs

Most wall and surface repairs take under 2 hours. Service calls start at $75, which includes the first 30 minutes, and every repair gets a professional finish — small doesn't mean sloppy.
