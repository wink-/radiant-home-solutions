Smart home devices are supposed to make life easier — but only if they're installed right and actually connected. We mount, wire, and configure them, then walk you through the app.

## What We Handle

- Smart thermostat installation
- Doorbell cameras and smart locks
- Smart switches and dimmers
- WiFi extender mounting
- Cable management and organization

## Good to Know

Most smart home installations take 1–3 hours. Service calls start at $75, which includes the first 30 minutes.

We'll also give you an honest read on your setup: if your WiFi won't reach the far corner of the house, we'll say so and mount the extender properly instead of selling you devices that will keep dropping offline.
