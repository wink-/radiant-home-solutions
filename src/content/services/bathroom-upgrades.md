You don't need a bathroom remodel to make a bathroom better. Small upgrades add safety, storage, and comfort — often in a single morning.

## What We Handle

- Toilet paper holders and towel bars
- Bathroom mirrors and medicine cabinets
- Grab bars and safety rails
- New toilet seats and bidets
- Shower caddies and organizers

## Good to Know

Most bathroom upgrades take 1–3 hours. Service calls start at $75, which includes the first 30 minutes. ADA-compliant installations are available — grab bars go into studs or with proper blocking, so they actually hold when needed.

Grab bars are one of the most valuable small jobs we do for senior homeowners and family members planning to age in place.
