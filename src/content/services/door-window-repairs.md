Doors that stick and windows that won't open properly are small jobs with a big daily payoff. Quick adjustments make everything smooth again.

## What We Handle

- Squeaky door hinges
- Sticky windows and sliding doors
- Door handle and lock adjustments
- Weatherstripping replacement
- Screen door repairs

## Good to Know

Most door and window repairs take 30 minutes to 2 hours. Service calls start at $75, which includes the first 30 minutes — and we often bundle several doors and windows into a single visit.

Weatherstripping is one of the best-value small jobs heading into Southern Tier winters: it seals gaps, cuts drafts, and lowers heating bills.
