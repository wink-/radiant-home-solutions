# Modern Kitchen Transformation

**Project Duration**: 3 weeks  
**Services**: Cabinet installation, countertop replacement, electrical work, plumbing

## The Challenge

The Johnson family contacted us about their outdated 1980s kitchen. The original oak cabinets were showing their age, the laminate countertops were chipped, and the electrical system couldn't handle modern appliances.

## Our Solution

**Week 1: Demolition & Prep**
- Safely removed old cabinets and countertops
- Updated electrical circuits for new appliances
- Relocated plumbing for new island layout

**Week 2: Installation**
- Installed custom white shaker-style cabinets
- Added under-cabinet LED lighting
- Installed quartz countertops with waterfall edge

**Week 3: Finishing Touches**
- New tile backsplash installation
- Cabinet hardware and trim work
- Final electrical connections and testing

## The Results

> "We couldn't be happier with our new kitchen! The team was professional, clean, and finished exactly on schedule. Our home value increased significantly, and we love cooking here now." - Sarah Johnson

**Key Features:**
- Increased storage with custom cabinet solutions
- Energy-efficient LED lighting throughout
- Durable quartz countertops with 15-year warranty
- Modern electrical system supporting all new appliances
